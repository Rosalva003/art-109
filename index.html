About

Art as a place to process, play, and grow.

I am currently finishing my Digital Media Art degree at San Jose State University. My work blends 3D modeling, animation, illustration, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to turn internal reflections into visual narratives.

Stars, soft colors, expressive characters, and dreamy details often appear in my work. They help me build a visual language around curiosity, self-identity, mental health, and the process of becoming.

Artist statement

I use digital tools as a sketchbook for feelings that are hard to explain. Each piece is a small world where technology, memory, and imagination can meet.
