About me

Storytelling through color, code, and feeling.

Hi, I am Rosalva! I am a Mexican digital artist currently in my final year at San Jose State University, majoring in Digital Media Art.

My work explores the intersection of technology and storytelling. I blend 3D modeling, animation, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and digital illustration to create meaningful visual experiences.

A lot of my practice is deeply personal, often moving through themes of self-identity and mental health to turn internal reflections into visual narratives.

Artist statement

Digital tools help me give feelings a shape.

Rosalva Jimenez is a Mexican digital artist in her final year at San Jose State University, where she is finishing her degree in Digital Media Art. Her work mixes personal reflection with the joy of making things, treating the digital world as a playground for color, light, and new ideas.

After starting out in traditional art, Rosalva shifted her focus to the digital frontier. Today, she builds her worlds using 3D modeling, animation, and code. For her, technology is not just a tool; it is a way to visualize feelings that are hard to put into words.

Whether she is coding an interactive site, editing a video, or crafting physical objects, Rosalva's practice documents growth. Stars often appear in her work as symbols of wonder, curiosity, and the ongoing journey of becoming.
