About Me

Hi, I’m Rosalva!

I’m a Mexican digital artist currently in my final year at San Jose State University, majoring in Digital Media Art. My work is all about exploring the intersection of technology and storytelling.

I specialize in blending 3D modeling, animation, and code (HTML, CSS, and JS) to create meaningful digital experiences. A lot of my work is deeply personal, often diving into themes of self-identity and mental health to turn internal reflections into visual narratives.

Artist Statement

Rosalva Jimenez is a Mexican digital artist in her final year at San Jose State University, where she’s finishing up her degree in Digital Media Art. Her work is a mix of deep personal reflection and the pure joy of making things. While she uses her art to navigate big topics like mental health and self-identity, she’s just as dedicated to the "fun" side of creation, treating the digital world as a playground for color, light, and new ideas.

After starting out in traditional art, Rosalva shifted her focus to the digital frontier. Today, she builds her worlds using a mix of 3D modeling, animation, and code (HTML, CSS, and JS). For her, tech isn't just a tool; it’s a way to visualize feelings that are hard to put into words. You’ll often find stars hidden into her work—symbols of wonder and the constant journey of "becoming" that follow her from project to project.

Whether she’s coding an interactive site, editing a video, or stepping away from the screen to craft physical keychains, Rosalva’s practice is all about documenting her own growth. She’s driven by a massive sense of curiosity and the belief that art should be a space where imagination can run wild. For Rosalva, every piece is a chance to capture a memory and celebrate the process of growing as both an artist and a person. ⭑.ᐟ
