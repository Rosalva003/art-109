About Me

Rosalva Jimenez is a digital artist who is all about building a world that feels uniquely her own. Her work is a mix of growth, color, and self-discovery, fueled by a love for exploring new perspectives and the way emotion translates onto a screen.

After moving away from traditional art to dive into the digital world, Rosalva now uses her practice to visualize feelings that are sometimes hard to put into words, like identity and mental health. She is fascinated by the digital future and loves using tech to create spaces where imagination and curiosity can run wild.

You will notice stars following her from piece to piece; for Rosalva, they are symbols of wonder and the constant journey of becoming. When she is not working on a digital drawing, she is usually designing websites, making crafts, or experimenting with 3D modeling and digital collages. For her, art is a way to document her own evolution through vibrant colors and light. ⭑.ᐟ
